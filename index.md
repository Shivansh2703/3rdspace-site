# 3rdspace support

3rdspace is a presence-based social app for adults in Canada. It shows you which of your people are at the hotspots you both share, while it's still worth walking over.

## Get help

Email [3rdspace.app@gmail.com](mailto:3rdspace.app@gmail.com). Tell us what happened and which version of the app you're using. Please don't send passwords.

## Delete your account

Open the You tab, go to Settings, and tap Delete account. Your account is removed right away. What is and isn't removed is explained in the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## Report or block someone

Open their profile, tap the menu, and choose report. You can block them in the same step. Blocking is instant and silent: they aren't told, and you both disappear from each other. We review reports daily. See the [terms of use](terms.md) for what we don't allow.
