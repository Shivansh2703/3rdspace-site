# Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-30.

3rdspace is a presence-based social app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. It applies to the 3rdspace app. The app is offered in Canada and is for people 18 or older only.

Questions or requests: [3rdspace.app@gmail.com](mailto:3rdspace.app@gmail.com).

## In short

- We collect what the app needs to work. Nothing more.
- We don't track you, show ads, sell your data or use third-party analytics.
- We never store your coordinates. We store which hotspot you were at, and when.
- Only friends see your name and photo. Strangers see a username and a mutual-friend count.
- A private safety log of past hotspot visits is kept for 12 months. No one can see it in the app, including you.

## What we collect

- **Email address.** To create your account and sign you in. We also hold your password in hashed form through our sign-in provider.
- **Name and username.** The display name and @username you choose.
- **Profile photo.** If you add one.
- **Account settings.** Your visibility choice (visible, lowkey or off), your per-space settings, your profile colour and whether notifications are on.
- **Your hotspot and when.** When you're at a hotspot, we record which hotspot and the time you arrived and left. Your phone works out where you are on the device. The app sends us a hotspot, never raw latitude or longitude. Live presence disappears when you leave or it expires.
- **On the way.** If you say you're on your way to a hotspot, we record the hotspot and your estimated arrival time until it expires.
- **Friends, spaces, events and hotspots.** Your friends and friend requests, the spaces you create or join, the events you create, follow or RSVP to, the hotspots you add (name and place), and the content you write for them (names, titles, times).
- **Blocks and restrictions.** Who you've blocked or restricted. When you block someone we keep a copy of their name and username so you can see your blocked list, even if they later delete their account.
- **Reports.** If you report someone, we keep the report, its reason, and who filed it and who it's about.
- **App build and reliability counters.** The app version and how often your presence was renewed, used only to keep presence working.
- **Push token.** An identifier from Apple that lets us send you notifications, if you allow them.

We don't read your contacts, and we don't collect your phone number, advertising identifier, health or financial data, or browsing history. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so the app never gets access to your whole photo library.

## Why we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, show the live map, let friends and spaces see each other, send notifications you've allowed, and keep people safe. We don't use it for advertising or profiling.

## Who sees what

- **Spaces.** You're only visible at a hotspot to people who share a space with you that the hotspot belongs to.
- **Friends.** Your name and photo are visible only to mutual friends.
- **Everyone else.** Strangers in a shared space see an ember: a username and a mutual-friend count. No photo, no name.
- **Lowkey.** You still count in crowd numbers but aren't named. Each space has its own setting, and one switch applies to all of them. If you're lowkey on an event or "on the way" you can still be named there, as those are intentions you chose to share.
- **Off.** You aren't shown anywhere, and you can't see other people's presence either. You can still see places, events and your friends list.
- **Blocks.** Blocking is instant and works both ways. The other person isn't told.
- **Notifications.** We never send a notification that says someone is at a place.

## The safety log

When you leave a hotspot, we add a record to a separate safety log: your account, the hotspot, when you arrived and left, and your visibility setting at the time. This log is used only to investigate serious safety incidents. No feature in the app reads it, and no user can see it, including you. Access requires a filed safety incident, and every access is itself recorded. Records are permanently deleted after 12 months.

Deleting your account does not remove these records. They stay until their 12-month deletion date.

## Deleting your account

Open the You tab, go to Settings, and tap Delete account. This removes your account and sign-in, profile, photo, live presence, friendships, space memberships, event responses, push token and queued notifications.

What remains after deletion:

- Safety log records, until 12 months after each visit, as described above.
- Reports you filed or that were filed about you, kept without your account identity attached.
- Spaces, events and hotspots you created, which stay without a creator.
- If you were blocked by someone, their blocked list may keep the name and username you had when they blocked you.

## Service providers

We use Supabase to host our database, sign-in, file storage and backend functions, and Apple to deliver push notifications. They process data for us to provide the app. We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't sell it. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Your data is stored by Supabase in Canada (Montréal, AWS ca-central-1).

## Canada and your rights

We handle personal information in line with Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA. You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent by deleting your account. Email [3rdspace.app@gmail.com](mailto:3rdspace.app@gmail.com). We'll respond within 30 days. If you're unhappy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

## Age

3rdspace is for people 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, tell us and we'll remove it.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and if the change is significant we'll tell you in the app. Continuing to use 3rdspace after a change means you accept it.
